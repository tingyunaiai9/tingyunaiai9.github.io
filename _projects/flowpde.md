FlowPDE studies how flow matching can be understood and justified in the context of PDE solving. The work connects generative modeling ideas with scientific machine learning, aiming to make neural solvers more interpretable and principled.

This project was conducted during my remote research internship with the Computational Design and Fabrication Group at MIT CSAIL, advised by Prof. Wojciech Matusik.

**Keywords:** neural PDE solvers, flow matching, scientific machine learning, generative modeling.
