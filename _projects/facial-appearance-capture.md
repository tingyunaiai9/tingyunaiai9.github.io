This line of work studies facial appearance capture under unconstrained, in-the-wild conditions. I contributed to projects on hybrid inverse rendering and learned delighting priors for recovering facial albedo and appearance from challenging observations.

The work is connected to my research at the Institute of CG and CAD, Tsinghua University, advised by Prof. Feng Xu.

**Related publications:** WildCap (CVPR 2026 Poster) and Learning a Delighting Prior for Facial Appearance Capture in the Wild (SIGGRAPH 2026).

**Keywords:** inverse rendering, facial appearance, albedo capture, digital humans.
