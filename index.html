About Me

I am a senior undergraduate student in the School of Software at Tsinghua University, pursuing a B.E. in Software Engineering.

My research focuses on computer graphics and 3D vision, particularly inverse rendering, 3D representations and generation, and physically grounded learning.

I am fortunate to be conducting research with Prof. Feng Xu at Tsinghua University and Prof. Shuang Zhao at UIUC. Previously, I worked with Prof. Wojciech Matusik at MIT CSAIL, Prof. Yuan Liu at HKUST, and Prof. Yuwang Wang and Prof. Qionghai Dai at Tsinghua University.
