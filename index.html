School of Software

Tsinghua University

Beijing, China

I am a junior undergraduate student in the School of Software at Tsinghua University, pursuing a B.E. in Software Engineering.

I am fortunate to be conducting research with Prof. Feng Xu at the Institute of CG and CAD, Prof. Wojciech Matusik at the Computational Design and Fabrication Group and MIT CSAIL, and Prof. Shuang Zhao at the Siebel School of Computing and Data Science. Previously, I worked with Prof. Yuwang Wang and Prof. Qionghai Dai at the Metaverse AI Interdisciplinary Innovation Lab, and with Prof. Yuan Liu and Prof. Wenping Wang at the Intelligent Graphics Lab.

My research interests include 3D vision, computer graphics, and machine learning.

I am currently seeking a PhD position starting in 2027 Fall, and welcome conversations about research fit and collaboration!
